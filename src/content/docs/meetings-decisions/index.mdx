## Meeting record shape

Each meeting or recording record can include:

- Title and date.
- Participants, using only information appropriate for the repository.
- External video link with access context.
- Summary and topics discussed.
- Decisions and action items.
- Related materials and records.

## Decision record shape

Each decision record can include:

- Context and decision question.
- Options considered.
- Outcome and rationale.
- Consequences and follow-up.
- Owner, status, date, and related links.

Use the meeting and decision templates in the source `templates/` directory. Do not commit videos or other heavy binary files.
